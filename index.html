About the model

A dementia-safety research project

A safe zone that knows the plan

HALO is a dementia-safety research project at Missouri S&T. A voice assistant turns everyday conversation into confirmed calendar events, and the Mobile Zone (MoZo) stretches the home geofence along the route to each confirmed appointment, only for that appointment's time window. This simulator compares that approach against static and learned-routine geofences, to see how well each one tells a planned outing from wandering.
